1. System Goal (What the system will do)

Goal:
The GIHON AI-Enhanced E-Commerce Platform is a web-based marketplace designed to provide a secure, intelligent, and personalized online shopping experience.
It enables customers to browse and purchase products, allows administrators and sellers to manage products and orders, and uses AI technologies to enhance search, recommendations, and customer interaction
Key objectives include:
Facilitating online buying and selling of products,
Providing AI-driven product recommendations and search,
Supporting secure payments and order tracking,
Offering administrative control, analytics, and system monitoring.

3. Functional Requirements (5 User Stories / Use Cases)
User Story 1 – User Registration & Login

As a customer,
I want to create an account and log in securely,
So that I can manage my profile, orders, and shopping activities.

User Story 2 – Product Browsing & Search

As a customer,
I want to browse products by category and search using keywords or natural language,
So that I can easily find products I am interested in.

User Story 3 – AI-Based Product Recommendation

As a customer,
I want to receive personalized product recommendations,
So that I can discover relevant products based on my behavior and preferences.

User Story 4 – Shopping Cart & Checkout

As a customer,
I want to add products to a cart and complete checkout using secure payment methods,
So that I can place orders safely and efficiently.

User Story 5 – Product & Order Management

As an administrator or seller,
I want to manage products, inventory, users, and customer orders,
So that I can efficiently operate and monitor the e-commerce platform.
