# Shopping Cart Management Algorithm

Associated Requirement: SR-6 (Shopping Cart Management)

Description:
The system shall manage the addition, modification, and removal of products in the shopping cart.

Algorithm: Add Item to Cart
BEGIN AddToCart
    INPUT productID, quantity

    FETCH productStock FROM inventory

    IF productStock < quantity THEN
        DISPLAY "Product out of stock"
        EXIT
    ENDIF

    ADD productID and quantity TO cart
    UPDATE cartTotal

    DISPLAY "Item added to cart"
END
